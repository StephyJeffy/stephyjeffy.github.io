## How to deploy

Replace the site files in your existing `stephyjeffy.github.io` GitHub repository with the contents of this ZIP's `stephyjeffy.github.io` folder, commit and push the changes to its published branch. Your original `.git` directory was deliberately excluded from this delivery ZIP; keep the `.git` folder in your local repository instead. Open `index.html` locally to preview the static site; third-party embeds and fonts may require a live connection.

## Gallery editorial choices

Only a selected subset of the previously shared sports and networking photos was added to the public gallery, while the images were curated across portrait sessions to avoid repetition. Source images from the uploaded archives have not been overwritten.
